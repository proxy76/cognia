# Product Overview

## What is Cognia?

Cognia is a mobile-first social learning platform that delivers short-form, expert-created educational content in a TikTok-like experience. Students and curious learners discover bite-sized video lessons, take quizzes, earn points, climb leaderboards, and connect with friends — all within a visually modern, neon-purple academic interface.

## Target Users

| Segment | Description |
|---------|-------------|
| **Primary** | Students looking for engaging, short-form academic content |
| **Secondary** | Lifelong learners who want to expand their knowledge |
| **Creators** | Domain experts (not necessarily teachers) who produce educational videos and quizzes |

## Value Proposition

- **For learners:** A personalized, fun, and social way to learn across academic topics — combining discovery, gamification, and community.
- **For creators:** A platform to share expertise through short videos and quizzes, build a following, and see how learners engage with their content.

## Primary Platforms

- **Android** — Jetpack Compose (primary)
- **iOS** — Kotlin-to-Swift interop with shared KMP modules (primary)
- **Web** — Compose Multiplatform (secondary, full-featured client)

## Core Experience

Two main feeds drive content discovery:

1. **For You** — Personalized feed based on onboarding preferences and ongoing behavior.
2. **Deep Dive** — Exploration feed surfacing topics outside the user's usual interests. Repeated engagement with Deep Dive topics may promote them into For You.

## Launch Scope (High Level)

The MVP delivers: authentication, AI-assisted onboarding, dual feeds, video upload and playback, quizzes (multiple choice and true/false), points and levels, badges, leaderboards, a friend and follow system, private chat with content sharing, a moderation workflow, creator licensing, basic creator analytics, and notifications.

## Business Model

Free at launch. No monetization features are included in the MVP.

## Design Direction

- **Visual style:** Ultra-modern neon purple
- **UX reference:** TikTok-like vertical feed navigation
- **Brand feel:** Academic
- **Default language:** English (localization planned for later)
